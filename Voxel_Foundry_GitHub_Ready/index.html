Choose a shape and set its dimensions. Sphere and circle lock equal diameters; ellipses are one block high. Half shapes keep the positive half along your chosen cut axis. Dimensions describe the full parent shape. Use an X or Z cut for a semicircular torus arch; Y makes a half-tube ring.

Layers: Y slices run bottom to top, X left to right, and Z back to front. Layer numbers start at the first occupied plane and stop at the last, including after flips or rotations. Half-shapes exclude the empty half of their parent grid. Hover coordinates still refer to the parent grid. 2D always shows the selected single layer, regardless of the 3D range. Arrow keys step through layers when you are not typing.

Navigation: Drag to orbit in 3D or pan in 2D. Shift-drag, right-drag, or the Pan button pans in 3D. Scroll or use + / − to zoom. Center view recenters the object and resets the zoom while keeping your viewing angle.

Orientation highlights: 90° marks the two center axes; 45° marks true diagonals through the parent shape’s center. Both follow the selected slice direction and appear on the same blocks in 2D and 3D. Amber marks axes, blue marks diagonals, and purple marks their overlap. Even dimensions use the two central rows or columns. These are visual guides, not additional blocks.

Counting: Hover a 2D cell for its coordinates. Brackets mark uninterrupted rows or columns of 5+ blocks when zoomed in. Dashed blue blocks are the previous layer; dashed coral blocks are the next.

Shape method: Blocks are sampled at their centers. Hollow curved shapes subtract a smaller concentric shape; wall thickness reduces each radius in block units. Half shapes offer an open or solid flat face. Open top uses a hollow shell; Solid top caps the opening. Half spheres and ellipsoids start as bowls; Flip Y turns them into domes. Counts are exact for the generated blocks. Large objects may take a moment to generate.

Radius inputs are half the full dimensions. Half-block radii preserve odd diameters; switching units does not resize the object. Half-shape dimensions describe the parent shape before the cut. Settings stay in this browser. No Minecraft connection is required.
